# One Line of Earned Pride — entry

A program/v1 entry for the make.fun "One Line of Earned
Pride" bounty. `main.py` writes one line to
`/output/entry.txt` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
when that variable is set).

The line:

> At 58, after the night shift, she finally passed the exam she had failed at 19 -- and framed the certificate beside her daughter's, at the same height.

Thirty-nine years in the earning; one exact hanging decision
in the claiming. See `CANDIDATES.md` for the full draft list
this winner was picked from.
