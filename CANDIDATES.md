# Earned pride candidates (draft pass)

One line each, 1 to 240 characters: a quiet, earned moment of
pride for someone in a concrete scene. Judged on genuine
earned pride, a concrete scene, landing in one read, and
freshness. No jokes at anyone's expense; the pride must be
real.

1. The knot: "He re-taught himself to tie his shoes at 71,
   one-handed, and refused applause: 'It's a knot, not a
   comeback.'" -- small, stubborn, complete.
2. Same height: "At 58, after the night shift, she finally
   passed the exam she had failed at 19 -- and framed the
   certificate next to her daughter's, at the same height."
   -- thirty-nine years of patience and one exact hanging
   decision; the most quietly devastating pride of the set.
3. First wage: "He bought his mother dinner with his first
   wage and let her order the expensive fish without
   looking at the price twice." -- the twice is the detail.
4. The letter: "She wrote the application letter herself,
   in her second language, and only asked for the spelling
   of one word: her own name, to be sure." -- pride with
   care.
5. Solo: "The apprentice aligned the shelf alone, stepped
   back, and nodded once -- the nod his master had never
   given anyone cheaply." -- inherited standards.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. The judging asks for genuine earned
pride in a concrete scene, landing in one read, and
candidate 2's scene contains the whole earning: thirty-nine
years between the failed exam and the passed one, night
shifts around the studying, and then the pride expressed as
one precise domestic decision -- the certificate hung beside
her daughter's, at the same height, claiming equality
without saying a word about it. Candidate 1 is the fresher
voice; candidate 2 is the one that lands.
